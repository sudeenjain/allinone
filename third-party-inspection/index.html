Independent Third-Party Audit Workflow

Strict conformance to national inspection protocols and ISO/IEC 17020 requirements:

1. Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI)

Comprehensive statistical random sampling of finished manufactured goods to verify dimensional tolerance, workmanship, packaging integrity, barcoding, and marking standards before dispatch.

2. During Production Inspection (DUPRO)

In-line quality assessment directly on the factory floor to identify raw material flaws, calibration drift, or process irregularities early in the production cycle before final packaging.

3. Container Loading Supervision (CLS)

Supervising container structural integrity, temperature seals, tally counting, secure palletized loading, and customs seal placement for domestic transport and international sea cargo dispatches.
