Laboratory Set Up

Establishing a laboratory is a critical step in ensuring that testing and quality control processes meet industry standards. We offer comprehensive laboratory setup services that include designing the lab layout, selecting appropriate equipment, and ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements. Our team of experts will work closely with you to understand your specific needs and create a lab environment that optimizes workflow efficiency and safety. We also provide guidance on proper waste management, storage solutions, and safety protocols. Our goal is to ensure that your laboratory is equipped to perform accurate and reliable testing, thereby supporting your efforts in obtaining ISI certification from BIS in India.

The BIS / ISI Certification Lifecycle

Our verified step-by-step roadmap from initial gap analysis to official ISI license issuance.

STEP 01

Standard Identification

Locating applicable Indian Standard (IS code), product manual, and mandatory testing parameters.

STEP 02

Lab Infrastructure

Installing Caltronics testing instruments, glassware, reagent setups, and safety infrastructure.

STEP 03

Calibration & Audit

Pre-audit verification, traceable calibration documentation, and trial test batches.

STEP 04

BIS Audit & Grant

Coordination with Bureau of Indian Standards inspection officers and receipt of ISI mark license.
