Laboratory Instruments Calibration Services

Accurate testing results depend directly on the precision of your laboratory instruments. Professional calibration by certified metrologists ensures statutory audit compliance, error-free production lines, and traceable calibration records for ISO, BIS, and NABL requirements.

Explore Caltronics Equipment

Certified Metrology Technicians

Our calibration services are performed by highly trained metrology technicians using state-of-the-art master reference standards. We systematically identify sensor drift, calibrate electronic components, and eliminate measurement uncertainty across all critical parameters.

Traceable Calibration Certificates

We provide exhaustive calibration reports and traceability certificates for your permanent quality records. Each certificate contains uncertainty budgets, environmental test data, and master instrument traceability valid for ISO, BIS, and export audits.

Extended Instrument Life

Regular preventive calibration schedules protect sensitive sensors, prevent unbudgeted equipment breakdowns, and maintain the integrity of quality control workflows across ceramic, chemical, pharmaceutical, and soil laboratories.
