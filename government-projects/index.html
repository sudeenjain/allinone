Gujarat Government Soil Health Card Scheme

Launched by our Hon'ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi, the Soil Health Card scheme aims to promote crop-appropriate nutrient management across agricultural lands. All In One Technology was entrusted by the Gujarat Government to set up and operate 10 state-of-the-art analytical laboratories dedicated to analyzing soil samples submitted by farmers across Gujarat.

Public Sector Footprint

Government Departments Served

Active supplier and turnkey contractor across multiple key state and central agencies:

Universities & Research Institutes

Equipping higher education chemistry, biotechnology, and agricultural science laboratories with precision analytical instrumentation, reagents, and fume handling setups.

State Agriculture Departments

Deployment of portable water & soil analysis kits (CAL-061), spectrophotometers, flame photometers, and micro-nutrient analysis workstations for agricultural officers.

Government Hospitals & Medical Labs

Supplying automated 3-part hematology analyzers (CAL-081), differential cell counters (CAL-019), blood roller mixers (CAL-020), and clinical diagnostic reagents.

Engineering Colleges & Technical Labs

Outfitting material science, civil engineering, chemical engineering, and environmental engineering testing setups in complete compliance with AICTE guidelines.

Public Works Department (PWD)

Civil construction material testing, aggregate testing, cement testing, and water quality determination systems for public infrastructure quality control.

GeM Portal Direct Procurement

Listed and authorized on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) with registered vendor credentials for rapid government agency tendering and procurement.
