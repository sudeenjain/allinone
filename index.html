Inspection Workflow Reference

Integrated Inspection & Certification Workflow

A seamless 5-stage methodology ensuring highest testing accuracy, statutory compliance, and international conformity.

Stage 01

Requirements & Gap Analysis

Comprehensive evaluation of testing standards, ISI certification prerequisites, space allocation, and instrument lists.

Stage 02

Turn-Key Laboratory Setup

Delivery and precision installation of Caltronics equipment, reagent benches, ventilation, and regulatory safety setups.

Stage 03

Testing & Personnel Training

Rigorous hands-on training for client lab technicians on operating protocols, ISI standard testing methods, and data recording.

Stage 04

Metrological Calibration & Verification

Precision instrument calibration using traceable reference standards, generating official calibration certificates and audit trails.

Stage 05

Third-Party Inspection & Certification Grant

Formal BIS audit coordination, third-party inspection report issuance, and grant of ISI mark / ISO conformity.
